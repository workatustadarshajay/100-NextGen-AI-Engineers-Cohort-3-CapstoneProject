The clinical agent team

Every stage has
a clear job.

Seven coordinated roles turn an uploaded report into a source-linked, reviewable decision-support draft. The clinician still owns the final call.

01INGEST

Report Analysis Agent

Extracts patient context, history, medications, observations, and range-aware lab flags from the uploaded report.

02RETRIEVE

Guideline RAG

Retrieves relevant local and trusted guideline excerpts so each generated insight has a source to inspect.

03ROUTE

Risk Router

Directs critical findings to an urgent review lane and standard cases to the normal review queue.

04GENERATE

Summary Agent

Turns the analysis and citations into a concise, clinician-facing summary grounded in the supplied evidence.

05PRIORITIZE

Recommendation Agent

Proposes one to six prioritized follow-up actions grounded in the retrieved guideline context.

06RECONCILE

Clinical Reconciliation Agent

Keeps structured findings and recommendations aligned after a reviewer edits the narrative summary.

07LEARN

Reviewer Feedback Loop

Records summary corrections and recommendation decisions as bounded quality signals for future workflow runs. Feedback informs quality; it never replaces clinical evidence.
